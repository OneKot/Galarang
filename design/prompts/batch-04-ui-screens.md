# Батч 04 — экран коллекции и ветки скиллов

Образцы для прикрепления: `ui/ui-inventory-screen.png` (язык UI),
`ui/ui-fusion-screen.png` (панели/кнопки). 3:2, 2–3 варианта, без надписей
(тексты — пустые таблички, реальные подпишем в игре).

## 1. Коллекция (индекс предметов)

Mobile game collection screen UI mockup in handcrafted diorama style: a large
carved wooden panel with brass corners and blue enamel accents on a blurred miniature
green-valley diorama background. The screen shows an equipment collection index: a big
grid of square item cells — discovered items shown as miniature carved wooden weapons
and armor pieces on rarity-colored cell backgrounds (grey, green, blue, purple, red,
gold); undiscovered cells are dark charcoal-grey with a black silhouette of the item
and a small golden question mark. At the top, a separate showcase row with two LARGER
cells: a pure black-background cell holding a dark smoky secret sword (fully visible,
no question mark) and a glowing white-background cell holding a radiant white sword.
Under the grid, a fusion-stage switcher strip showing the same sword in four stages:
steel, gold, white-blue crystal, purple nebula. One cell is selected and an info card
opens on the right: a name plate, a blank carved plate for odds digits, a blank plate
with a small globe icon for world-count digits, and small wooden tabs. Warm golden
light, miniature scale, tilt-shift. No faces, no eyes, no people, no animals, no
skulls, no crosses, no religious symbols, no text, no letters, no numbers. 3:2.

## 2. Ветки скиллов

Mobile game skill tree screen UI mockup in handcrafted diorama style: carved wooden
panels with brass corners and blue enamel on a blurred miniature diorama background.
Layout: a winding stone path across the panel connecting round wooden skill nodes with
small carved icons: a four-leaf clover, a small chest, a winged boot, a golden chest,
a pickaxe, an axe, a fishing rod, a garden hoe, and four small elemental shields (fire,
poison, ice, lightning). Learned nodes glow gold with laurel borders, locked nodes are
dim grey with a small padlock, one node is highlighted with a tooltip card: a blank
name plate, level pips, a cost row with a coin icon on a blank plate, and a blue
upgrade button. At the top, available skill points shown as golden stars with a blank
counter plate. Bottom tabs: crossed swords, pickaxe, fish. Warm light, miniature
scale, tilt-shift. No faces, no eyes, no people, no animals, no skulls, no crosses,
no religious symbols, no text, no letters, no numbers. 3:2.
