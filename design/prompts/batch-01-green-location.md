Handcrafted tabletop diorama aesthetic, macro photography: a world built from real
moss, carved wood, natural stone and stitched fabric, everything looking like a handmade
miniature scale model. Strong tilt-shift depth of field, warm golden-hour sunlight,
ultra-detailed materials (wood grain, moss fibers, stone texture, woven fabric).

Жёсткие запреты (шариат, для всех промптов): no faces, no eyes, no mouths, no skulls,
no animals, no orcs/goblins, no crosses, no religious symbols, no pentagrams, no text,
no letters, no logos.
